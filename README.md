# power-bi-hybrid-employee-presence-tracker

## 📌 Project Overview

The Workforce Presence Insights Dashboard is a Power BI project designed to analyze employee attendance, Work From Home (WFH), and Sick Leave (SL) trends. The dashboard provides HR teams and management with actionable insights into workforce availability, employee presence patterns, and hybrid work behavior.

Using interactive visualizations and KPI tracking, this project helps organizations monitor attendance performance and make data-driven workforce planning decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Managing employee attendance and hybrid work arrangements has become increasingly important for organizations. HR teams need visibility into:

* Employee attendance trends
* Work From Home utilization
* Sick Leave patterns
* Workforce availability across different periods
* Employee presence behavior by day of the week

This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing a centralized view of workforce presence metrics.

## 🔍 Findings

### Attendance Trends

* Attendance was highest in April (94.0%).
* Attendance dropped to 89.7% in May.
* Attendance recovered to 91.7% in June.

### Work From Home Trends

* WFH reached its highest level in May (11.2%).
* Employees showed a stronger preference for remote work during periods of lower office presence.

### Sick Leave Trends

* Sick Leave increased significantly from 0.4% in April to 1.7% in May.
* Increased absenteeism contributed to the decline in attendance.

### Day-Wise Behavior

* Mondays and Tuesdays generally recorded the highest attendance.
* Fridays consistently showed lower attendance rates.
* Employees demonstrated a clear preference for flexible work arrangements near weekends.
